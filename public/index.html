The Problem

Students are applying blind.

Information about courses, admission requirements, and institutions is scattered across dozens of websites — making it difficult and time-consuming for students to compare options effectively. The result? Students miss opportunities, apply to the wrong programs, or simply give up.

Not Meeting Requirements

Students often don't meet admission requirements for programs they apply to — because they didn't have easy access to that information upfront.

Too Few Applications

Many students apply to too few institutions, significantly limiting their opportunities and backup options.

No Centralized Platform

There is no single place in South Africa to compare admission requirements across institutions and make truly informed decisions.

The Solution
